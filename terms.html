1. Agreement

By adding Asbern (“the Bot”) to a Discord server, or by using it in a server where it is installed, you agree to these terms. If you do not agree, do not use the Bot. These terms are between you and legal or trading name (“we”, “us”).

You must also comply with Discord’s own Terms of Service and Community Guidelines. Nothing here overrides them.

2. What the Bot is

The Bot is community-management software for Discord servers, designed to be run by a server owner on their own hardware. It provides activity levels, an in-server virtual economy and games, a shared watch-party feature for media the server owner already possesses, game-server controls, statistics, an administrative console, and an optional voice AI companion.

3. Who is responsible for what

  • The server owner installs and runs the Bot, chooses which features are on, configures which channels it uses, holds the data it stores, and is responsible for how it is used in their server.
  • We provide the software.
  • You are responsible for your own conduct in the server.

4. Acceptable use

You may not:

  • use the Bot to break Discord’s Terms of Service, Community Guidelines, or any law that applies to you;
  • attempt to exploit, manipulate or automate the economy or games, including with alternate accounts, scripts, or by abusing a bug rather than reporting it;
  • attempt to gain administrative access to the Bot or the machine it runs on;
  • interfere with the Bot’s operation for other members, including by flooding, spamming or deliberately triggering rate limits;
  • use the Bot’s watch-party feature with material you do not have the right to play to the people you are playing it to.

We or the server owner may restrict or remove your access at any time for breaching these terms.

5. The virtual currency and the games

The Bot’s currency and casino games are entertainment features inside a Discord server. Specifically, and without exception:

  • The currency has no real-world value.
  • It cannot be purchased with real money.
  • It cannot be exchanged, redeemed, cashed out or transferred for anything of real-world value.
  • It is not a security, a token, a deposit or a payment instrument.
  • Balances, items and progress may be adjusted, reset or lost as a result of bugs, moderation, data loss or changes to the Bot, and no compensation of any kind is owed if that happens.

The games are games of chance for entertainment only and are not gambling services.

This is the same rule the product page states and the code enforces: a subscription buys access to features. It never grants currency, alters a balance or changes odds.

6. Media, game servers and third-party services

  • The Bot’s screening-room feature plays media from a media library the server owner already operates. We do not supply, host, index, distribute or help acquire any media, and the Bot is deliberately not connected to any download or acquisition tooling. What is played, and whether it may lawfully be played to that audience, is entirely the server owner’s responsibility.
  • The Bot can start and stop game servers on the operator’s own machine. Those games are governed by their own publishers’ terms.
  • The Bot can talk to third-party services the operator configures — for example a media server, a statistics provider, or an AI provider. Those services have their own terms, and we are not responsible for them.

7. The AI companion

The optional AI companion is off by default. When a server owner turns it on and adds you to its allow-list:

  • Its output is generated by a language model and may be wrong, inappropriate or nonsensical. Do not rely on it for anything that matters.
  • Its replies are not advice of any kind.
  • You can mute it, opt out of profiling, and delete everything it holds about you, at any time, from the Bot’s menu.

8. Availability

The Bot is provided as is and as available. Because it is self-hosted, it is online only when the machine running it is online. We do not guarantee availability, uptime, data preservation or that any feature will continue to exist.

To the maximum extent the law allows, we are not liable for any indirect, incidental, special, consequential or exemplary damages, or for any loss of data, progress, virtual currency, profits, or server availability, arising from your use of the Bot.
