Billing & usage

Your subscription buys features. Media egress and AI are billed on what you actually use, as separate lines — because a cheap subscription cannot also quietly pay for other people's bandwidth. Both default to off, both are capped by a number you set, and when a cap is reached the feature stops rather than the invoice growing.
