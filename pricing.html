The three rules
  • No base fee. No setup fee, no per-seat charge. The plan price is the whole fixed cost.
  • Metering defaults to off. The failure mode is the feature stopping, never an invoice you did not expect.
  • Nothing is deleted to make you pay. Lapsing makes paid modules read-only; balances and levels are untouched.

Usage-based, separately

Two things are metered, and both default to off.

A subscription cannot be both cheap and quietly pay for somebody else’s movie night. So the two costs that genuinely scale with use sit outside the plan — and both are built so that the failure mode is the feature stopping, never a surprise invoice.

If a payment fails

Nothing is deleted to make you pay.

A lapsed server’s economy is never zeroed, reset or recalculated. The data is yours; it is the access that lapsed. The same is true of downgrading — the stats collector keeps writing history your plan cannot yet read, so upgrading later reveals all of it instantly rather than starting you from today.

The same principle governs member limits. If your server outgrows a band we tell you and invite you up. A member ceiling is never a kill switch — switching off a 12,000-member community’s economy because it recruited well is how you generate a public incident.
