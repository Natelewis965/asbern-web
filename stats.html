Member detail

Rankings and server totals are public here. A member's hour-by-hour profile is not — it is only ever shown to that member.

What is deliberately missing

No message content, ever. Word clouds, "most used words", emoji rankings and sentiment are not here and are not planned. The collector has stored counts and timings only since 2022 and no part of it has ever held a character of message text. Adding them would mean a new collector, a privacy policy that does not exist yet, and reading everything everyone writes.
No online/idle/offline status and no "what everyone is playing". Both need Discord's Presence privileged intent, which this bot does not request. Without the intent there are no events, so a status chart could only ever be a drawing.
No invite attribution. Who invited whom needs an invite-code cache diffed on every join. Nothing collects it today, so "top inviters" would be an empty table with a confident heading.
